The problem space

Real workplace challenges.
Practical HR responses.

Good HR technology should help people, not just create more dashboards. This concept connects common workplace challenges with simple, measurable actions.

🧭
Challenge 01

New hires feel lost

New employees may not know what to do, who to ask, or how to measure success during their first month.

Solution idea: a 30-60-90 day onboarding checklist, buddy support, and weekly check-ins.

🌱
Challenge 02

Skills are not growing

Employees may not know which skills to build or which learning resources connect to their career goals.

Solution idea: a skills-gap review, recommended learning plan, and manager follow-up.

💬
Challenge 03

Employee feedback gets missed

Teams may collect feedback without turning it into visible action, reducing trust and engagement.

Solution idea: short pulse surveys, anonymous themes, action owners, and progress updates.

📊
Challenge 04

HR decisions lack evidence

People decisions can rely too heavily on assumptions instead of clear, relevant measures.

Solution idea: a transparent HR scorecard with attendance, learning, retention, and engagement context.

🤝
Challenge 05

Hiring feels slow or unclear

Candidates and hiring managers may experience delayed updates and inconsistent interview steps.

Solution idea: a shared hiring-stage tracker, structured interview rubrics, and clear candidate communication.

🛟
Challenge 06

Wellbeing concerns appear late

Workload issues may remain hidden until they affect performance, attendance, or retention.

Solution idea: voluntary check-ins, workload conversations, support pathways, and privacy safeguards.

From problem to action

A simple HR problem-solving framework

Use this process to show an HR manager that you can move from understanding a problem to evaluating a solution.

1
Listen to employees

Gather appropriate feedback and understand the real pain point.

2
Define the problem

Describe who is affected, when it happens, and what success would look like.

3
Choose a small intervention

Test a practical change before scaling it across the organisation.

4
Measure and improve

Compare appropriate before-and-after measures and ask employees what changed.

Responsible HR technology

Technology should support people — not label them.

PeopleFirst is a concept project designed to demonstrate thoughtful HR problem-solving.

  • Use only authorised and privacy-protected data.
  • Never use an automated score alone to make hiring, promotion, or termination decisions.
  • Provide transparency, accessibility, human review, and a way to raise concerns.
  • Do not present sample dashboard figures as real company performance.

People-first thinking. Data-informed action.
